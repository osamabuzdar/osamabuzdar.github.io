Start Here

U Drive support helps authorized vehicle owners, drivers, fleet users, and business users get help with the mobile app and vehicle tracking service. If something is not working as expected, gather your registered phone number or account ID, vehicle name or registration number, device model, app version, and a short description of the issue before contacting support.

For urgent vehicle safety concerns, contact your local emergency services first. U Drive support can help with app access, service status, tracking data, and product troubleshooting.

Help Articles

Sign in to U Drive Make sure you are using the registered account credentials provided for your vehicle or fleet. If login fails, confirm your internet connection and request account verification from support.
View live vehicle status Open the vehicle list or map view to check online status, speed, ignition, and location. If data looks delayed, refresh the app and check mobile data or Wi-Fi.
Review trips and reports Use trip history, mileage, alarms, and reports to review vehicle movement. Availability may depend on your tracking device, service package, and account permissions.
Manage alerts Vehicle alerts and push notifications may require notification permission in iOS settings. Confirm alerts are enabled for U Drive and that your account has access to the selected vehicle.

Resolve Product Issues

Try these steps first for common app and tracking problems:

  1. Confirm your iPhone has a stable internet connection.
  2. Close U Drive completely, then open it again.
  3. Check the App Store for the latest U Drive update.
  4. Confirm your account is assigned to the correct vehicle or fleet.
  5. If the vehicle is offline or data is delayed, wait a few minutes and refresh the vehicle screen.
  6. Contact support with screenshots, error messages, vehicle details, and the time the issue happened.

Account and Access

  • If you cannot sign in, verify that your account is active and that you are using the correct credentials.
  • If a vehicle is missing, ask your fleet administrator or U Drive support to confirm vehicle assignment.
  • If your account should be removed, contact support and include the account identifier used for U Drive.
  • For business or fleet accounts, access changes may require approval from the account owner or administrator.

Tracking and Vehicle Data

Vehicle location, ignition state, speed, mileage, route history, and alerts depend on your assigned tracking device, network coverage, server availability, and account permissions. If vehicle data appears unavailable, delayed, or incorrect, support may ask for the vehicle registration number, last known location, device status, and approximate time of the issue.

Some issues may require checking the installed tracking hardware, SIM/network connectivity, vehicle power, or backend service configuration.

iOS App Checklist

  • Update U Drive from the App Store when a new version is available.
  • Open iPhone Settings, choose U Drive, and confirm notification access if you want vehicle alerts.
  • Make sure Low Data Mode, VPN, or restricted network settings are not blocking app connectivity.
  • Restart your iPhone if maps, reports, or notification delivery continue to behave unexpectedly.

Contact Customer Service

If the help articles do not solve your issue, contact U Drive customer service. Include your name, registered account or phone number, vehicle name or registration number, iPhone model, iOS version, app version, screenshots if available, and a clear description of the problem.
