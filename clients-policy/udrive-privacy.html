Overview

U Drive provides vehicle visibility tools such as real-time vehicle status, map tracking, trip history, mileage information, dashboard summaries, alerts, reports, and support workflows. To deliver these features, the app may process account details, vehicle data, device information, usage activity, notification identifiers, and support information.

We use this information to provide and improve U Drive, protect accounts, show vehicle-related information to authorized users, troubleshoot issues, and communicate important service updates.

Information We Collect

Account information Login credentials, user identifiers, session details, selected language, preferences, and other information required to verify access to the service.
Vehicle and tracking data Vehicle registration/name, status, speed, ignition state, location, mileage, trip routes, alarms, reports, and related fleet activity.
Device and app data Device type, operating system, app version, IP address, diagnostics, crash or error logs, connectivity status, and app configuration.
Notifications and support Push notification tokens, notification interactions, complaints, support requests, and contact details you provide when asking for help.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users and keep accounts protected.
  • To show authorized users live vehicle location, speed, status, trip history, reports, alarms, and dashboard data.
  • To send important vehicle alerts, promotional notices when enabled, service messages, and app update information.
  • To provide customer support, handle complaints, investigate reported issues, and maintain service reliability.
  • To analyze app performance, fix errors, improve map and reporting flows, and protect the app from misuse or unauthorized access.

Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when needed to operate U Drive, provide maps, deliver notifications, maintain infrastructure, process support requests, or comply with legal obligations.

Services that may process information

  • Google Maps and related map services for map display, routes, and location visualization.
  • Firebase services, including Firebase Cloud Messaging, for notifications, delivery diagnostics, and app infrastructure.
  • Google Play Store and Apple App Store for app distribution, updates, and platform-level services.
  • Hosting, analytics, logging, and backend service providers used to keep vehicle tracking and reporting features available.

App Permissions

U Drive requests permissions only when required for app features. Internet access is required to connect to U Drive services, retrieve vehicle data, load maps, and deliver notifications. The app may open phone dialer, browser, sharing, or external map apps when you choose related actions.

Vehicle locations shown in U Drive generally come from the tracking service and assigned devices. If a feature asks for device-level permission in the future, the app will use it only for the feature described at the time permission is requested.

Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide U Drive features, maintain account records, support vehicle history and reports, comply with legal or business requirements, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service configuration.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You may stop using the app at any time.
  • You may contact support to ask about your account information, vehicle access, correction requests, or deletion requests where applicable.
  • You can manage some app preferences, language options, and notification settings inside the app or through your device settings.
  • You can review external service privacy practices through their official privacy pages.

Children's Privacy

U Drive is intended for authorized vehicle owners, drivers, fleet users, and business users. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through U Drive, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of U Drive after changes are posted means the updated policy applies to your use of the service.
