Service business SEO works best when the fundamentals are calm, accurate, and consistent. Search engines need to understand who you serve, what you offer, and which pages deserve attention.

## Give every service a focused page

One generic services page is usually not enough. Create focused pages for web development, mobile app development, WordPress, digital marketing, and other core offers.

## Use specific page metadata

Titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, and social previews should match the content of each page. Generic metadata across the whole site weakens the signal.

## Keep performance visible

Large images, heavy scripts, and layout shifts can hurt both users and rankings. Static rendering, optimized assets, and careful component boundaries help keep pages fast.

## Publish useful articles

Blog content should answer real customer questions, not just fill a calendar. Practical comparisons, implementation guides, and common mistakes can support both search and sales conversations.
